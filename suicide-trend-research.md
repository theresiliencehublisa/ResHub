# Australian suicide trend chart — source notes

Checked 31 October 2026. The latest ABS release is *Intentional self-harm (suicide) deaths, 2025*, published 27 October 2026. The national annual series below was extracted from ABS 2025 Data Cube 11: Table 11.1 for registered death counts and Table 11.7 for age-standardised death rates (Australia, persons). The 2025 ABS release summary independently reports 3,283 initially coded deaths and an age-standardised rate of 11.6 per 100,000.

| Registration year | Registered deaths | Age-standardised rate per 100,000 | Data status |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2016 | 2,939 | 12.0 | Final |
| 2017 | 3,292 | 13.2 | Final |
| 2018 | 3,205 | 12.7 | Final |
| 2019 | 3,377 | 13.2 | Final |
| 2020 | 3,184 | 12.2 | Final |
| 2021 | 3,220 | 12.3 | Final |
| 2022 | 3,342 | 12.6 | Final |
| 2023 | 3,311 | 12.2 | Revised |
| 2024 | 3,326 | 11.9 | Preliminary revised |
| 2025 | 3,283 | 11.6 | Preliminary |

ABS footnotes say these statistics are by date of registration; 2016–2022 are final, 2023 is revised, 2024 is preliminary revised, and 2025 is preliminary. The 2025 count is expected to increase as coronial cases are finalised. Age-standardised rates are per 100,000 estimated resident population and use the Australian 30 June 2001 standard population. The ABS cautions that recent-year figures may be revised and year-to-year comparisons should be interpreted carefully. Label both chart series clearly and show the preliminary status beside recent data; do not imply that a population-adjusted rate is a raw count.

Use careful, non-sensational language and place crisis support nearby. The existing site already displays crisis contacts; repeat a concise 000/Lifeline 13 11 14 note next to the graph, and link readers to Mindframe's guidance.
